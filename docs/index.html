Frequently Asked Questions

How does Gajalogic achieve sub-millisecond latency while running Post-Quantum Cryptography? +

Gajalogic implements zero-copy memory ring buffers and hardware-accelerated lattice mathematics (ML-KEM / ML-DSA). Cryptographic handshakes and digital signatures execute asynchronously alongside the distributed saga pipeline, maintaining an internal switch P99 latency of <0.82ms.

What is the "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) threat and why must banks act today? +

Adversary state intelligence networks continuously intercept and archive encrypted banking data across public telecommunication lines. Long-lived identifiers (account numbers, Aadhaar linkages, historical transaction records) remain sensitive for decades. When Cryptanalytically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQCs) arrive, classical RSA/ECC will be cracked instantaneously. Gajalogic neutralizes this threat by sealing data in quantum-intractable lattice envelopes today.

How does Gajalogic eliminate legacy per-TPS licensing taxes? +

Legacy switches like Base24 and Way4 charge tiered per-transaction licensing penalties that escalate exponentially as UPI volume grows. Gajalogic provides an elastic compute-capacity model running on standard Kubernetes and commodity hardware—meaning zero per-transaction penalties regardless of whether your bank processes 1 million or 100 million transactions daily.

Can Gajalogic integrate seamlessly with our existing Core Banking System (CBS)? +

Yes. Gajalogic supports plug-and-play ISO 8583, ISO 20022, and modern REST/gRPC interfaces compatible with Infosys Finacle, TCS BaNCS, and Oracle Flexcube. Its in-memory shadow ledger intercepts 84.6% of read and balance queries, directly shielding core banking databases from lock contention and high-concurrency crashes.

Is Gajalogic compliant with NPCI and Government of India PQC Task Force mandates? +

Yes. Gajalogic natively adheres to all NPCI UPI, RuPay, and NCMC specifications while proactively aligning with the Government of India Post-Quantum Cryptography Task Force (mandating PQC foundations for Indian BFSI by 2027) and upcoming RBI Q-SAFE standards.
