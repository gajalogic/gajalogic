Architecture Blueprint

Decoupled Cryptographic Gateways & Autonomous Routing

Eliminate the dilemma between mobile app bundle size and enterprise-grade post-quantum cryptographic security.

Pillar 01 // Decoupled Cryptographic Sidecar

Isolate PQC Complexity from Mobile Clients

Post-quantum key encapsulation (ML-KEM) and signatures (ML-DSA) require non-trivial key sizes (up to 1,568 bytes) and cryptographic compute. Gajalogic decouples PQC key management into high-speed gateway sidecars, keeping consumer mobile app APK/IPA sizes featherlight.

Stateless Token Exchange Sidecar PQC Key Broker NIST FIPS 203 Handshake
Pillar 02 // Multi-Bank PSP Traffic Mesh

Eliminate Sponsor Bank Outage Blackouts

When a partner sponsor bank suffers a connectivity dip or degradation, Gajalogic dynamically shifts traffic across secondary and tertiary PSPs in real-time without session teardown or consumer app error prompts.

Autonomous PSP Health Probes Predictive Route Hedging Sub-Second Traffic Shedding
Pillar 03 // Real-Time Regulatory Policy Push

Instant Compliance without Mobile App Updates

NPCI regularly introduces circulars regarding handle migrations, interchange mandates, and payment limits. Gajalogic allows TPAP product teams to update routing policies and regulatory flags dynamically from the cloud without shipping app store updates.

Dynamic Policy Engine Instant Over-The-Air Config Automated Circular Audit
Pillar 04 // High-Concurrency Intent Routing

Sub-Millisecond Deep-Link & Intent Handling

Purpose-built to ingest millions of concurrent UPI Intent calls generated by e-commerce checkout flows, ridesharing apps, and food delivery platforms with low CPU memory overhead and zero queue stalls.

Zero-Copy Intent Ingestion Lock-Free Token Bucket High-Yield Redis Cluster Fabric
Resilient Infrastructure // Zero App Downtime

Active-Active Sponsor Bank Load Balancing

TPAPs cannot afford to rely on a single sponsor bank. A temporary downtime at one partner bank during payday or evening peaks can wipe out tens of millions in GMV.

Gajalogic orchestrates an active-active mesh across up to 12 sponsor bank PSPs. Real-time synthetic pings measure round-trip latency, error code spikes, and NPCI decline indicators every 50 milliseconds—shifting intent payloads away from degraded banks in under 400 milliseconds without dropping in-flight sessions.
